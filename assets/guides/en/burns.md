# BURNS

Burns
Cool, cover, and know when to get urgent help.

---

## Get urgent help for

**Call emergency services for large burns, burns to the face, hands, feet, groin, or over joints, deep or charred skin, and any chemical, electrical, or smoke-inhalation injury.**

Also get help for any serious burn on a child or an older adult.

## First steps

1. Move the person away from the heat source. Put out flames if clothing is on fire (stop, drop, and roll).
2. Cool the burn with **cool running water for at least 10 minutes**, up to 20 if possible.
3. Gently remove rings, watches, and tight clothing near the burn before it swells. Do not pull off clothing stuck to the skin.
4. Cover the burn loosely with a clean, non-fluffy cloth or clear plastic wrap.
5. Keep the person warm, but do not let the burned area get cold.

## Do not

- Do not use ice or very cold water.
- Do not apply butter, oil, toothpaste, egg, or any home remedy.
- Do not pop blisters.

## Chemical burns

- Brush off any dry powder first, then flush the skin with plenty of running water for at least 20 minutes. Remove contaminated clothing while flushing.

## Electrical burns

- Do not touch the person until the power is switched off. Even if they look fine, get medical care. Electrical injuries can affect the heart.
