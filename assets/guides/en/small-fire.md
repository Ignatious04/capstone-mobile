# SMALL FIRES

Small Fires
When to fight a fire, and when to just leave.

---

## Decide quickly

**Only fight a fire if it is small, you have an extinguisher, and you have a clear exit behind you. If in doubt, get out and call for help.**

Leave right away if the fire is spreading, the room is filling with smoke, or the extinguisher runs out.

## Using a fire extinguisher (PASS)

1. **Pull** the pin.
2. **Aim** low at the base of the fire, not the flames.
3. **Squeeze** the handle.
4. **Sweep** from side to side until the fire is out.

Stand about 2 meters away and keep your back to your exit.

## Cooking oil or grease fire

- Turn off the heat if you can do it safely.
- Cover the pan with a lid or a baking tray to smother the fire. Leave it covered until it is completely cool.
- **Never pour water** on an oil fire. It will cause the fire to flare up.

## Electrical fire

- If it is safe, switch off the power at the breaker or unplug the appliance.
- **Never use water** on an electrical fire. Use an extinguisher meant for electrical fires.

## After the fire

- Even if you put it out, call emergency services so they can check for hidden fire.
