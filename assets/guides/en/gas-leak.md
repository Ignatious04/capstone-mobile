# GAS LEAK (LPG)

Gas Leak (LPG)
Steps to take if you smell gas.

---

## Signs of a leak

**If you smell gas or hear hissing, do not create sparks or flames. Do not touch light switches or electrical appliances.**

Cooking gas has a strong, unpleasant odor, often compared to rotten eggs.

## What to do

1. Put out any open flames, cigarettes, or candles.
2. Open doors and windows to let the gas out.
3. If you can reach it safely, turn off the valve on the gas tank.
4. Get everyone, including pets, out of the house and away from the area.
5. Call emergency services from a safe distance outside.
6. Do not go back in until responders say it is safe.

## Do not

- Do not switch lights or appliances on or off.
- Do not start a vehicle or use a lighter near the leak.
- Do not try to find the leak with a flame.

## If someone feels unwell

- Dizziness, headache, nausea, or trouble breathing can be signs of gas exposure. Move the person to fresh air and tell the call-taker.

## Prevention

- Check hoses and connections for cracks, and keep the tank upright and away from heat.
