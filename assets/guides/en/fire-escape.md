# ESCAPING A FIRE

Escaping a Fire
How to get out safely, or what to do if you are trapped.

---

## Get out first

**Leave immediately. Do not stop to collect belongings. Call emergency services only once you are outside.**

## Getting out

1. Shout "Fire!" to alert everyone, then leave right away.
2. Stay low. Smoke rises, and cleaner air is near the floor.
3. Before opening a door, feel it with the back of your hand. If it is hot, do not open it. Use another way out.
4. Close doors behind you to slow the fire.
5. Do not use elevators. Use the stairs.
6. Once you are out, **stay out**. Go to your meeting point and count everyone.

## If your clothes catch fire

- **Stop**: do not run.
- **Drop**: get down to the ground and cover your face with your hands.
- **Roll**: roll back and forth until the flames are out. Then cool the burn with running water.

## If you are trapped

1. Close the door and block the gaps with wet cloth or towels.
2. Go to a window. Open it if it is safe, and signal for help with a bright cloth or a flashlight.
3. Call emergency services and tell them exactly where you are.
4. Stay low and keep calm. Do not hide in closets or under beds.

## Prepare ahead

- Agree on two ways out of every room and a meeting place outside.
- Test smoke alarms if you have them, and keep exits clear.
