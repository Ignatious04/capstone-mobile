# BREAK-IN OR ROBBERY

Break-In or Robbery
Stay safe if someone is breaking in or has entered your home.

---

## Your safety comes first

**Do not confront the intruder. Property can be replaced. People cannot.**

## If someone is trying to get in, or is inside

1. If you can leave safely, get out and go to a neighbor or a safe place. Call the police from there.
2. If you cannot leave, go to a room that locks. Lock the door and move away from it.
3. Call the police. If speaking is unsafe, stay on the line or use the app to send a report.
4. Keep quiet and keep your phone on silent.
5. Tell the police how many people are in the house, and where.

## If you are confronted

- Stay calm, do not resist, and do what they ask.
- Do not make sudden movements. Keep your hands visible.
- Try to remember details such as height, clothing, voice, and any weapon.

## If you come home and think someone broke in

- Do not go inside. A broken window or an open door can mean the intruder is still there.
- Go somewhere safe and call the police from outside.

## Afterwards

- Do not touch or clean anything until police have seen it.
- Write down what was taken and what you remember while it is fresh.
