# CPR (HANDS-ONLY)

CPR (Hands-Only)
For an adult who is unresponsive and not breathing normally.

---

## Call for help first

**Call emergency services now and put the phone on speaker. The call-taker can guide you step by step.**

## Check the person

1. Make sure the area is safe for you.
2. Tap their shoulders firmly and shout, "Are you okay?"
3. Look at the chest for up to 10 seconds. If they are not breathing, or only gasping, start CPR.

## Do chest compressions

1. Lay the person on their back on a firm, flat surface.
2. Kneel beside their chest. Place the heel of one hand on the center of the chest, and your other hand on top.
3. Keep your arms straight and your shoulders directly over your hands.
4. Push hard and fast: about **5 cm (2 inches)** deep, at **100 to 120 compressions per minute**.
5. Let the chest rise fully between pushes.

## Keep going

- Do not stop until responders take over, the person starts breathing normally, or you are too exhausted to continue.
- If someone else is with you, swap every 2 minutes so the pushes stay strong.
- If an AED (automated defibrillator) is nearby, send someone to get it. Turn it on and follow its voice instructions.

## Good to know

- This guide is for adults. For a child or infant, tell the call-taker right away. The technique is different and they can coach you.
- You may hear or feel ribs crack. This can happen and should not stop you.
- Acting is better than waiting. Hands-only CPR can keep blood flowing until help arrives.

**This guide does not replace training. Consider taking a certified first-aid and CPR course.**
