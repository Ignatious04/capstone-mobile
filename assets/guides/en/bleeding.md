# SEVERE BLEEDING

Severe Bleeding
Stop heavy bleeding while waiting for help.

---

## Call for help

**Call emergency services if blood is spurting, soaking through cloth, or will not stop.**

## Control the bleeding

1. If you can, use gloves or a clean plastic bag to protect your hands.
2. Press **firmly and directly** on the wound with a clean cloth, gauze, or clothing.
3. Keep pressing without lifting to check. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top. Do not remove the first layer.
4. Have the person lie down and keep the injured part still.
5. Once the bleeding slows, bandage the pad firmly in place. It should be snug but not cut off circulation.

## If something is stuck in the wound

- Do not pull it out. Press around it and pad on both sides to hold it steady.

## Only if direct pressure fails

- For life-threatening bleeding from an arm or leg that does not stop, a tourniquet may be used. Place it about 5 to 7 cm above the wound, not over a joint, and tighten until the bleeding stops.
- Note the time it was applied. Do not loosen or remove it. Leave that to medical staff.

## Watch for shock

Signs include pale, cold, clammy skin, fast breathing, dizziness, confusion, or weakness.

- Keep the person lying down and covered with a blanket or jacket.
- Do not give food or drink.
- Stay with them and keep talking to them until help arrives.
