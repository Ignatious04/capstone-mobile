# IF YOU WITNESS A CRIME

If You Witness a Crime
What to note and what to avoid while you wait for police.

---

## Stay safe

**Do not chase or confront anyone. Move to a safe place and call for help.**

## What to note

Try to remember, or write down as soon as it is safe:

1. What happened, where, and at what time.
2. How many people were involved, and whether anyone has a weapon.
3. What they looked like: approximate height and build, clothing, hair, and anything distinctive.
4. Any vehicle: color, type, plate number, and direction it went.
5. Which direction the person left in.

## While you wait

- Keep other people away from the area.
- Do not touch or move anything that could be evidence.
- If someone is injured and it is safe to help, tell the call-taker so a medical response can be sent.
- Stay reachable in case police need more information.

## Afterwards

- Write down everything you remember while it is fresh.
- Be ready to give police a statement.
- You can also file a report in the app so that the details are on record.
